Name Days Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Name Days (package com.bkapp.namedays) does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The app works fully offline: it does not request the Android INTERNET permission and cannot connect to the network.

  • No personal information is collected or transmitted.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs are used.
  • No account or login is needed.
  • All name day calendars are bundled inside the app.

Data kept on your phone

These items stay on your device only and are never sent to the developer or anyone else:

  • Your settings: chosen countries, language, theme, reminder time.
  • Names you watch and names you add yourself (“My names”).
  • Edited wish texts, if you change the templates.

You can remove all of this at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. If Android's device backup is turned on for your phone, Google may include the app's local data in your own Google backup; the developer has no access to it.

Contacts (optional)

If you turn on contact matching, the app asks for the Android Contacts permission. It then reads first names on your device to show which contacts celebrate a name day this week. Contact data is held in memory only while the app is used, is not saved by the app, and is never uploaded or shared. You can decline or revoke the permission at any time; the rest of the app works without it.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are local notifications created on your phone. The home screen widgets read the app's bundled data and settings on your device. The app uses the “receive boot completed” permission only to restore reminders and refresh widgets after a restart.

Sharing wishes

When you tap share on a name day wish, the text is handed to the app you choose in Android's share sheet (for example WhatsApp or SMS). What happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy. Name Days itself sends nothing.

Purchase

Name Days is a paid app. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play; the developer receives no payment information and no personal data about buyers beyond what Google Play provides in aggregate sales reports. The app has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
